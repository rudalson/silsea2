## Sunlight

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production 2D game sprite animation atlas for a cute original alicorn named Sunlight. The user's hand-drawn reference features a friendly horse with a single spiral horn and feathered wings with SUN emblems at the feather tips. Preserve that identity. Design an adorable but heroic small pony, rounded cream ivory body, warm apricot and golden flowing mane/tail, honey gold hooves, large expressive amber eyes with highlights, blush cheeks, little smile. Feathered ivory/gold wings, three clear tiny sun emblems at outer feather tips. Polished storybook CEL CARTOON, colored dark brown outlines, two or three flat shading tones, readable silhouette, no gradients. All sprites face RIGHT in side view. 
EXACT layout: transparent background PNG, 4 columns by 4 rows equal square cells, 16 fully separated sprites, generous transparent gutters. No text, grid, captions, scenery, shadows on ground or loose sparkles. Consistent same character proportions and scale across all cells. Each full pony including horn, wings, hooves, tail must fit its cell.
Row 1: standing idle with folded wing; same standing blinking eyes shut; crouched landing; recoiling hurt eyes squeezed.
Row 2: four distinct sequential gallop poses with alternating extended and tucked front/back legs, wings folded.
Row 3: jumping legs tucked; falling legs reaching down; flying wing fully raised; flying wing stretched horizontally.
Row 4: flying wing lowered; swimming with legs paddling; joyful rearing victory front hooves raised; guarding with large wing curved forward.
Wings and horn are always present. Wing tip SUN motifs must be clear. Actual transparent alpha background. Beautiful crisp finished game art, not a rough sketch.
